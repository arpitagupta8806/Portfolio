Core Features — MoSCoW Prioritized
Real-Time Budget & Expense Tracking (Must Have) — Budget breakdown by category, live burn chart, and an approval workflow for every expense submitted.
Vendor Directory & Payments (Must Have) — Maintain a vendor directory, track payments, manage contracts, and rate vendor performance across events.
Team Roles & Task Management (Must Have) — Invite team members via email, assign roles and tasks, track progress in real time, and never miss a deadline.

Snapmint — Trust, Discovery & Safety
Core Thesis

Snapmint doesn't have a credit problem. It has a trust and experience problem. Users who complete one transaction have every reason to return — but the experience gives them every reason not to.

Impact Metrics
270
Highest RICE Score
+20%
Target Checkout Conv.
Zero
CIBIL Incidents (Hard Req)
5 Features Across 3 Phases
F1 · Credit Limit Visibility (RICE 270) — Always-visible credit dashboard + UPI deposit top-up. Pure UI, zero new backend. +20% checkout conversion target.
F2 · Stores Revamp (RICE 115) — 3 separated tabs: Partner Sites / Vouchers / In-App Shops. Pre-redirect guides and real-time integration status.
F3 · Snapmint Assured (RICE 84) — Merchant trust certification. EMI success ≥92%, delivery ≥90%, AI-generated scorecards. Recalculated monthly.
F4 · EMI Dispute Flow (RICE 112) — In-app dispute with instant auto-freeze, CIBIL protection, 3-level escalation including RBI officer. Zero CIBIL impact = hard requirement.
F5 · Price Lock + Cart + Bundle EMI (RICE 42) — Persistent cart, Bundle EMI with tenure selector, 3–5 day price lock. No penalty on expiry.
A Trade-off I Stand By

"Chose the in-app dispute flow over faster support. Faster support can't auto-freeze EMIs or protect CIBIL in real time. An automated pipeline reduces support dependency permanently."

— PM Decision Rationale

AI Agent Orchestration Platform — Enterprise Chatbot
The Work

Product Manager owning an internal AI platform at Innova Solutions end-to-end — full PM responsibilities from requirements through delivery, plus leading a UI/UX team on the design side.

Requirements & Documentation — Gathering requirements across stakeholders and writing the specs and PRDs that drive delivery.
Wireframes & UI/UX Leadership — Producing wireframes and directing a UI/UX team to turn requirements into shipped screens.
AI Architecture — Agentic AI Chatbot

An Agentic AI system: a router/orchestration agent delegates each query to specialized sub-agents (names confidential), including an AI skill-matching agent, combining Retrieval-Augmented Generation, Text-to-SQL, and LLM summarization into one production chatbot.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) — Semantic search over Elasticsearch feeds relevant context into the response.
Text-to-SQL — Natural language converted to SQL, with self-healing query correction and safety guardrails against malicious queries.
LLM Summarization — A summarization agent synthesizes retrieved data into the final natural-language chatbot response.
PM Ownership — Defined the orchestration logic, agent boundaries, guardrails, and success criteria across the system.
